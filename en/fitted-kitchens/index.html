Service — Fitted kitchens

The kitchen — the heart of every home.

A bespoke fitted kitchen, thought through from cooking and living — not from the floor plan. Concept, material and installation from a single source, for a kitchen that works today and still convinces in twenty years.

Approach

The wall disappears. The kitchen becomes the center.

Switching from a closed wall kitchen to an open kitchen island is one of the most common wishes for a new fitted kitchen: more room to cook together, a natural gathering point for guests, and a seamless transition to the living area. We plan your island with an integrated cooktop, as a pure work and display surface, or as a combination of both.

Craft

Designed without handles. Down to the last detail.

Matte lacquer fronts, warm real-wood veneer, handle-less systems with push-to-open: the front defines your kitchen's character. Together with you, we develop a front concept that suits your home — attending to every detail, from panel height to the transition into the countertop.

Material — Dekton

Dekton by Cosentino. The countertop that fears nothing.

Sintered at over 1,200 °C, making it virtually non-porous: a hot pot straight off the stove can be set down without a trivet, without harming the material. It's also UV- and frost-resistant — suitable for outdoor kitchens too — and available in large-format slabs for nearly seamless kitchen islands.

Dekton — Technology

How natural stone is formed. In hours, not millennia.

Raw materials made from glass, quartz and porcelain are fused under extreme pressure and over 1,200 °C into a homogeneous, ultra-compact material — a process that mimics natural stone formation, only vastly accelerated. The result: a virtually non-porous surface that gives bacteria no foothold and lets stains from red wine, oil or lemon simply wipe away.

Dekton — Highly versatile

Not just the countertop. One material for indoors and out.

Dekton comes in over 30 colors and surface finishes — from marble and concrete looks to pure metal tones — and not just as a countertop: also as a backsplash, cabinet front, or on the patio as an outdoor kitchen, thanks to its weather resistance. The result is one continuous material story from the cooking area all the way outside.

Appliances & built-in technology

Built in, not bolted on. One coherent picture.

Oven and steam oven at eye level, an induction cooktop with integrated extraction, a built-in fridge-freezer without a visible front: we select and coordinate the right appliances for your everyday life — matched for brand, ease of use and energy efficiency, not fitted in as an afterthought.

Materials & Quality

From the front's lacquer to the carcass. Every material chosen with care.

Alongside Dekton, we favor solid wood for warm, long-lasting fronts and quartz composite as a robust alternative for countertops. Every material is chosen together with you based on use, look and budget — heat-, stain- and scratch-resistant, built for decades rather than years.

Kitchen layouts at a glance

Which layout suits your home?

Together, we'll work out which kitchen layout fits your floor plan and cooking habits:

  • L-shape — two work axes at a right angle, plenty of storage with short distances
  • U-shape — three work axes surround the cook, maximum storage for larger kitchens
  • With kitchen island — a freestanding island as the centerpiece of open-plan kitchens
  • Single-row — all elements along one wall, space-saving for compact floor plans

About Richard Atelier

Human-centered living design — rooted in craft since 1935.

Richard's path begins in a Viennese carpentry tradition founded in 1935 by Leopold Dobrohruschka and still alive today in its third generation. Design at Richard Atelier and the precise craftsmanship at Tischlerkultur come from one coordinated hand — so nothing gets lost between the first sketch and the finished kitchen. “Spaces that aren't just built, but experienced.” — Richard Dobrohruschka
