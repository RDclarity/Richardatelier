Service — Bathroom renovation

The bathroom — the most personal room in the home.

A complete bathroom renovation, thought through from the person — not from the tile. Concept, material and execution from a single source, so your bathroom returns to entirely new brilliance in a short time: durable, hygienic, unmistakably yours.

Approach

More than a functional room. A continuation of your retreat.

Your bathroom is far more than just another room — it is the daily extension of your bedroom, the first and the last place of the day. We don't begin with the floor plan, but with your everyday life: how does your morning start, how does your evening wind down? A careful analysis of space, light and movement turns it into a bath that is functional and comfortable, and at the same time matches your style and the atmosphere of your home.

Craftsmanship

Conceived seamlessly. Down to the last detail.

Function and aesthetics belong together. Robust, large-format surfaces with minimal joints create a bath that is hygienic and easy to care for — and feels calm, because the eye never stumbles. Ceramic is the ideal material for damp rooms: a porosity of almost zero means that neither stains nor water can penetrate, for more hygiene with less maintenance.

Material — Ceramic

Innovative, elegant and made for a lifetime.

Ceramic combines character with permanence: heat-, stain- and scratch-resistant, impact-resistant, moisture- and UV-proof — indoors and out. A material you choose once and needn't touch again for a long time.
