Service — Fitted wardrobes

The wardrobe — more than just storage.

A bespoke fitted wardrobe, thought through from your actual wardrobe — not from the floor plan. Concept, material and installation from a single source, for storage that works today and still convinces in twenty years.

Approach

The freestanding wardrobe disappears. The walk-in closet takes over.

Switching from a freestanding wardrobe to a walk-in closet is one of the most common wishes for a new fitted wardrobe: better visibility of your entire wardrobe, more efficient use of space, and a dressing experience like in a boutique. We plan your walk-in closet as an open system, with floor-to-ceiling sliding doors, or as a combination of closet wall and walk-in area.

Craft

Every inch considered. Down to the last detail.

Pull-out trouser and tie racks, open compartments for bags and shoes, soft-close drawers: we plan the interior layout around your actual wardrobe, not catalog dimensions — for a wardrobe that uses every inch.

Material — Hardware & fronts

Quiet, sturdy. Built for a lifetime.

Solid wood, lacquered fronts and high-quality hardware with soft-close doors and drawers: sturdy, quiet-closing and built to last decades — not years. A material you choose once and won't need to touch again for a long time.
