Interior Architect (m/f/d)

Richard Atelier curates living spaces — Human-Centered Living Design from Vienna, working between Vienna and New York. We don't design rooms; we design the way people live: from penthouses to villas to executive offices. At the center is a small, precise team with a high standard of quality.

Requirements

  • Completed degree in interior architecture or comparable training
  • Confident use of CAD/3D software (e.g. AutoCAD, SketchUp, Vectorworks)
  • Experience developing high-end residential or commercial concepts
  • Understanding of material, light and spatial effect

Qualifications

  • Design sensibility combined with technical understanding
  • Independent, structured way of working from concept to execution
  • Enjoyment of close collaboration with clients and craft businesses
  • Fluent German and English, written and spoken
