Accounting (m/f/d)

Richard Atelier curates living spaces — Human-Centered Living Design from Vienna, working between Vienna and New York. We don't design rooms; we design the way people live: from penthouses to villas to executive offices. At the center is a small, precise team with a high standard of quality.

Requirements

  • Completed training in accounting/bookkeeping
  • Professional experience in ongoing bookkeeping, ideally in services or the trades
  • Confident use of accounting software (e.g. BMD, RZL or similar) and MS Excel
  • Knowledge of Austrian tax and levy law a plus

Qualifications

  • Accuracy and a strong grasp of figures
  • Independent, structured way of working
  • Discretion when handling sensitive data
  • Reliability and punctuality
