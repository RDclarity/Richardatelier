The curator

Richard translates personality into space.

A curator is neither a salesperson nor a decorator. Richard listens, orders, selects — and brings light, wood, floor, colour and form together into a concept that grows out of your life, not out of a catalogue. He remains the single point of contact, from the first sketch to the finished handover.

Origins

Raised between workshop and drawing board.

Richard's path begins in the craft: in a Viennese carpentry tradition founded in 1935 by Leopold Dobrohruschka and carried on today in the third generation. Between workbench and floor plan, Richard learned that good design never ends with how something looks — it has to hold, to function, and to last for decades.
